# Welcome to Cabinet

<span class="tx-accent">**Your Knowledge Base, your AI Team.**</span> Local-first. Markdown-backed. Yours.

Cabinet collapses your notes, files, tasks, and AI agents into <mark data-color="yellow">**one folder on your computer**</mark>. You open one app — everything is there. You close your laptop — your AI team keeps working.

## Cabinet in three ideas

**<span class="tx-amber">One folder for everything.</span>** Notes, PDFs, spreadsheets, code, diagrams, embedded apps — first-class viewers, no juggling tools. <span class="tx-green">If it lives in a folder, Cabinet can show it.</span>

**<span class="tx-purple">An AI team that lives next to your work.</span>** Hire personas. Give them skills. Schedule tasks, routines, and heartbeats. Their outputs land back in your folder as <mark data-color="green">durable pages</mark> — not lost in a chat transcript.

**<span class="tx-accent">Yours, on disk, forever.</span>** Plain markdown plus a tiny manifest. Git-trackable. Bring your own AI provider — <mark data-color="yellow">Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, or a local model</mark>. No database. No cloud. No lock-in.

## Browse a ready-made AI team

<mark data-color="amber">**[cabinets.sh](https://cabinets.sh)**</mark> is a public registry of plug-and-play cabinets — a complete AI team for a job hunt, a solo startup, a wedding, a podcast, a property portfolio. Clone one folder, run one command:

```bash
npx cabinets add <owner>/<template-name>
```

<span class="tx-green">Open it in Cabinet. Every agent, every job, every page is already there.</span> Edit a persona, change a schedule, make it yours.
